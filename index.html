Soạn Thảo Văn Bản

⠿
hueej (huệ) is an experimental type (rainbow) family designed to reinterpret the unique linguistic mechanics of Vietnamese queer slang. The typographic architecture is drawn from a Vietnamese queer lexicon Chỉ Bàn Lộn (Vol. 01 & 02) by Đinh Thị Nhung.
Default screen fonts are engineered to make us read as fast as possible to keep up with digital life. “hueej” refuses clean, corporate legibility and rejects being consumed instantly.

Just as using queer slang is an act of hiding taboo, intimate meanings so that it requires cultural literacy and intimate contextual knowledge to decode. Based on that idea, “hueej” forces the reader to pause, linger, and actively decode the characters to find their meaning - transforming the writing & reading into a slow, protected sanctuary for linguistic expression, subcultural memory, and intimate communication.
